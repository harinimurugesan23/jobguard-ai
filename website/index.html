What JobGuard AI Does

🔍 Scam Risk Detection

Analyze job offers and recruiter messages for potential warning signs associated with recruitment scams.

🚩 Red Flag Detection

Identify signals such as upfront payment requests, suspicious links, unrealistic salaries, urgency, and sensitive information requests.

🛡️ Safety Recommendations

Receive practical recommendations about what to verify before continuing with a job opportunity.

📊 Risk Assessment

Get a simple risk level and score together with an explanation of the signals found in the provided message.

How It Works

Paste a job offer, recruiter message, email, WhatsApp message, or other employment-related communication into JobGuard AI. The system analyzes the information provided and identifies potential warning signs, positive signals, and details that should be independently verified.


JobGuard AI is designed as a safety-awareness tool. Its assessment is not definitive proof that a recruiter, company, or job opportunity is fraudulent. Users should independently verify employment opportunities before taking action.
