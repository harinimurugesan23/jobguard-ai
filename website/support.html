JobGuard AI Support

Welcome to the JobGuard AI support page. This page provides information about using the application and understanding its safety assessments.

How can JobGuard AI help?

JobGuard AI analyzes job-related messages and identifies potential warning signs such as payment requests, sensitive information requests, suspicious links, unrealistic promises, urgency, and unusual recruitment behavior.

My analysis looks incorrect

JobGuard AI provides an automated risk assessment based only on the information supplied by the user. A risk assessment should not be treated as definitive proof that a recruiter, company, or job opportunity is fraudulent.

If an assessment seems incomplete, provide more relevant context about the job message and independently verify the recruiter, company, job posting, and official communication channels.

Safety reminder

Never share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, card details, or other highly sensitive information with an unverified recruiter. Do not make recruitment-related payments unless you have independently verified the opportunity and understand the reason for the payment.
