Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JobGuard AI handles information provided while using the application.

Information You Provide

JobGuard AI allows users to provide job-related content such as recruitment messages, job descriptions, emails, or other employment communications for analysis.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, card information, government identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Purpose of Analysis

Information submitted to JobGuard AI is used to generate a safety assessment of the provided job-related content. The assessment may identify potential warning signs, positive signals, and information that should be independently verified.

Data Minimization

Users should provide only the information necessary to evaluate a job opportunity. Avoid including unnecessary personal or confidential information in messages submitted for analysis.

Third-Party Services

JobGuard AI operates within the Anna platform and may use platform services required to provide AI-powered analysis. Information handled by such services may be subject to their applicable policies and terms.

Security

Users should independently verify recruitment opportunities and should never provide passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, card details, or other highly sensitive information to an unverified recruiter or job opportunity.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the JobGuard AI application or its data-handling practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page.

Contact

For questions about JobGuard AI, please use the support information provided on the JobGuard AI support page.
